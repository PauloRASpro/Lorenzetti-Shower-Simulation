Source-generated plotting functions may emit multiple panels. The full-resolution PNGs and the recalculated numerical tables are written beneath `results/replayed/<study>/original_figures/`; the notebooks provide inline previews. The coverage report distinguishes generated outputs from missing experimental inputs. Rendering a numerical figure is not by itself evidence of numerical equivalence to a published figure without the historical archive and matching software environment.
